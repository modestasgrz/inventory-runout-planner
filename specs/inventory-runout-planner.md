# Inventory run-out planner

Status: approved (Change 3 + follow-up) | Date: 2026-11-10 | Owner: Modestas

## Goal
A purchasing manager today keeps a spreadsheet to answer "what runs out, when, and what do I reorder within this month's budget". This app replaces that spreadsheet for a generic retail/wholesale catalogue: real public monthly demand for 36 products plus clearly labelled synthetic stock data, a what-if scenario sidebar, and a ranked reorder plan where every number can be traced to its formula and inputs.

## In scope
- Build script: download UCI Online Retail II (sha256-checked), clean it, select 36 products, write the committed derived demand CSV; seeded synthetic stock data written to a committed CSV.
- Pure calc core (Pydantic models + pure functions, no Streamlit): forecast, do-nothing projection, run-out month, limiting factor, reorder suggestion, ranking, budget funding, KPIs, CSV export tables, number formatting.
- Streamlit app: pages Overview, Forecast, Scenarios, Reorder plan, Methodology; shared sidebar with scenario inputs, 5 presets and Reset; KPI strip on every page; 4 CSV downloads; dark navy theme (one theme file, `.streamlit/config.toml`, one CSS file, one Plotly template).
- Input validation with clear messages; synthetic-data notice, planning date and dataset attribution on every page.
- `requirements.txt` exported from `uv.lock` for Streamlit Community Cloud.
- Playwright screenshot tool (1280 and 1440 px) and a README with screenshots, method and attribution.

## Out of scope
- Uploading your own product or stock data, editing products in the app, saving scenarios between sessions.
- Multi-order plans (the plan is the next order per product; shortfalls after its cover are only flagged, DEC-19), multi-echelon / multi-warehouse, supplier price breaks, currency conversion.
- Statistical forecasting models (ETS, ARIMA, ML), confidence intervals.
- Deploying to Streamlit Community Cloud (later owner step), any outbound action.
- A monthly cash budget view; thousands separators inside the sidebar number inputs (Streamlit limit).
- Any LLM or external API call at runtime.
- Aligning the theme with the sibling app `retail-kpi-dashboard` (owner proxy does it after both builds).

## Acceptance criteria

Hand-calculated cases (C6) use toy products and a flat history of 60 units in each of the 24 history months unless stated. Months are indexed 1 = 2011-12 (Dec 2011), 2 = 2012-01, … Toy product defaults: `unit_cost=2.00, stock_on_hand=100, lead_time_weeks=4, moq=50, capacity=400, inbound_qty=0, inbound_weeks=0, group="Gifts & small goods"`. Toy scenario defaults: `horizon_months=6, growth_pct=0, delay_weeks=0, budget_gbp=1000, safety_months=1.0, method="seasonal_naive"`.

Data
- **AC-01** — Given the committed `data/demand_monthly.csv`, when it is read, then it has exactly the columns `stock_code, description, group, month, units`, exactly 36 distinct `stock_code`s × 24 months `2009-12`…`2011-11` = 864 rows, every `units` is an integer ≥ 0, every product has `units > 0` in at least 22 of the 24 months, and the 36 codes are the top 36 by total units among products meeting that rule under the DEC-03 cleaning.
- **AC-02** — Given the committed `data/demand_monthly.csv`, when its `group` column is read, then every value is one of `Bags & storage`, `Candles & lights`, `Baking & party`, `Gifts & small goods`, assigned by the DEC-04 keyword rule from the description.
- **AC-03** — Given the committed `data/products_synthetic.csv`, when it is read, then it has exactly the DEC-05 columns, one row per stock code in `demand_monthly.csv`, every row passes the `Product` model, and regenerating with `synthetic.generate(history, prices, names, seed=42)` (history and names from `demand_monthly.csv`, prices from `data/median_prices.csv`) returns identical rows.
